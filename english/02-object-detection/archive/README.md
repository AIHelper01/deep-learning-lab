The Chinese course keeps two additional archived items: the PaddleYOLO-era theory and practice notes in
`../../../02-object-detection/archive/lessons/`. They are Chinese-language historical courseware and are not
translated, because their commands, external repositories, and environments no longer exist in this course.
The original Chinese YOLO11/PaddleYOLO notebook is no longer archived: at the owner's request it now sits at
`../../../02-object-detection/notebooks/01-yolo11-rock-paper-scissors.ipynb` as read-only course history. It drives
the external PaddleYOLO project, so it is never executed and has no English twin; that treatment is registered in the
`HISTORY_IN_NOTEBOOKS` and `ONE_SIDED` tables of `scripts/check_parity.py`.
The English equivalents of the current lessons are in `../lessons/`.
